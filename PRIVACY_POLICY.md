# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Baller Grid is developed by BeMs. Contact: aondoalumun27@gmail.com.

## Information Stored on the Device

The game stores progress, unlocked levels, stars, scores, coins, lives and regeneration timestamps, sound/music/vibration/notification preferences, tutorial completion, and daily challenge date/streak in browser or Android WebView local storage. This information stays on the device and is not sent to a game server. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may erase it. The game does not require an account.

## Advertising

The source includes an optional Google AdMob integration for banners, interstitials, and rewarded ads. Development uses Google's official test ads. If production ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may collect or process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information described in Google's own policies. Ads may not load when offline. The game does not grant rewards for ad clicks.

Google's consent-management platform (UMP), provided through the AdMob plugin, is invoked before production ad requests. The availability and content of consent choices can vary by region and applicable requirements. Users can reopen available privacy options from the in-game Privacy screen.

## Analytics and Third Parties

No analytics SDK or game-server tracking is included. Google AdMob is the only optional third-party service currently integrated. If analytics or other services are added, this policy must be updated before release.

## Retention and Choices

Local game data remains until the player resets progress, clears app/browser storage, or uninstalls the app. Advertising data handling and retention are controlled by Google and described in Google's privacy documentation. Users may disable ads only where the app's production configuration and applicable consent choices permit; core gameplay remains available without network access.

## Children and Contact

The developer has not configured child-directed or age-restricted ad treatment. Set the appropriate AdMob settings and review applicable requirements before distribution. For privacy questions, contact [Developer contact email].
